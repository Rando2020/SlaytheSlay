# Oathbound: adversarial balance audit v0.6

Status: proposed rules, not production changes. Basis: Master Card Library v0.4 and Fun-First v0.5. The README and indexed source currently describe a combat-stance implementation (76 HP, Pilgrim Star, 22 cards), so this audit is about the proposed redesign rather than verified defects in the game. No code, simulation or playtest executed. All numerical examples are hand calculations with stated assumptions.

## Priority findings and interventions

| ID | Risk | Example / impact | Decision / smallest intervention | Side effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B01 | Critical: singleton Sanctum | One Hand of Salvation (1E, 7 Block, 2 Anointed, 2 HP once per fight) is guaranteed every eligible draw. Six automatic draws yield 42 Block, 12 Anointed granted before cap, 2 HP. Alternating yields 21 Block, 6 Anointed, 2 HP. More divine cards dilute it. | Preserve specialization but test an available/spent state in each Sanctum, refresh spent at next turn start after an actual ordinary reshuffle. Automatic draw replaces one of five normal draws; fallback normal if none. Drawn cards become spent on return whether played or not. | Small decks and ordinary draw acceleration become attractive; short fights may see fewer replays. |
| B02 | Critical if healing unguarded | Repeated Salvation vs 6 damage/turn, 10 plays = 20 HP healed if unguarded. Existing once-per-instance rule prevents it. | Healing allowance follows original instance through transformations, restore and recall; first-play healing explicitly printed, never repeat. Varran conversions share it; authored native divine damage is not restricted. | First play at full HP may waste heal; Aurel overheal can grant Block. |
| B03 | Critical conditional: Energy/retrieval loop | Grace of Aurel (0E, consumes Anointed, +1E and Divine Draw 1) with Salvation (1E, +2 Anointed) becomes net Energy-neutral and generates Block/status if a repeatable retrieval path closes the loop. Two cards alone do not close it; existing guards prevent. | Keep once-per-combat evolved Offering Energy grant; other legal effects may resolve. All retrieval respects eligibility; no current-resolution self draw. | Spent Energy card may be a weaker draw; display spent effect. |
| B04 | Earned burst | Price of Victory pays 2 HP and offers a card for +2E, 1 Bloodlust. 3E becomes 5E; Godbreaker 2E, Warmonger 1E, Strike 1E leave 1E. | Preserve burst, do not nerf solely for magnitude. | Needs high-tier investment and opportunity cost. |
| B05 | Critical conditional: sacrifice/Restore | Funeral Rite + Soulbound + Echo of Death can Offer, restore, and Offer same instance. Requiem counts unique Exhausted IDs; Energy allowance never resets. | Allow bounded repeat Offering, exclude Restore-capable cards and Powers from Restore; Echo of Death Exhausts and cannot restore itself. No recursive automatic triggers. | Less freedom for exotic recursion; direct curated exceptions possible. |
| B06 | High: deck thinning | A 10-card normal deck draws 50% on a 5-card turn; after two pledges, 8 ordinary cards and normal-only draw 62.5%. Abandoning deity makes those evolved cards dormant; normal inventory was already smaller, so switch itself does not remove them again. | Pledge opportunity replaces a reward; preview normal and accessible deck sizes. Prototype existing 8 ordinary minimum as a temporary gate, compare a softer rule allowing pledge only when enough ordinary cards remain for two useful hands; no free cards or automatic release on switch. | May make first two pledges consume all starter headroom; test early reward cadence. |
| B07 | High: Anointed | Salvation grants 2 each draw; one hostile Weak application each turn leaves 1 additional stack before cap. With every-turn singleton access, enemy debuff identity disappears. | Fix access cadence before nerf. One stack per separate hostile application, block entire stack count; cap 3 in v0.4. Self costs and divine penalties bypass. Enemies should have mixed threats, not artificial immunity. | Debuff-heavy encounters may remain favorable, which is desirable. |
| B08 | Moderate: Bloodlust/multi-hit | 2-hit attack at 6 per hit becomes 18 if +3 applied per hit, versus 15 if +3 once per card. | Consume one Bloodlust per damaging Attack card, add +3 to first hit, before normal attack modifiers; no trigger on divine damage Skills. | Multi-hit builds less explosive but Strength still synergizes. |
| B09 | High: Resolve/retaliation | 9 Block + 2 Resolve vs one 15-damage hit: 6 leaks, one Resolve prevents 3, 3 HP lost, one stack remains. Against three 5-damage hits: first two spend 9 Block, Resolve prevents 1 leak on second hit and 3 on third, 2 HP lost. | Resolve consumes one stack per leaking attack hit, no spill or negative damage. v0.5 removes passive retaliation; if tested, only actual Block absorbed counts and cap across enemy turn. | Different hit patterns can produce different outcomes; must preview. |
| B10 | High: scaling and stalling | Conqueror's Will grants 1 Strength on first voluntary HP loss each turn; 5 triggers yield +5 combat Strength for at least 10 HP if using 2-HP costs. Passive enemies invite stalling. | Strength resets each combat; HP cost must be paid, not blocked; enemies escalate through readable intents. Keep once/turn trigger. No arbitrary global Strength cap. | Slow defensive players may face pressure; tune encounters. |
| B11 | High: switching reward farm | Repeatedly accept first-oath Pledge or loyalty upgrade if shrine can be revisited; free progress and small deck. | Rewards tied to unique event ID and consumed once. First oath bonus once per run; no mid-combat switching in initial prototype. Preview dormant inventory; rare mortal release forfeits tier. | Fewer switch opportunities; intentional returns remain meaningful. |
| B12 | High: dual oaths | Aurel + Varran would heal and damage if modifiers branch off the same source; Orun + Mora can preserve and Offer from multiple piles. | Conversion replaces source, not clones. Both share a single automatic slot and same refresh cycle. Divine Draw uniformly selects eligible card instances across active piles unless card names a god. Native status effects work across gods; relics explicitly change access. | Aurel overheal does not trigger on Varran conversion, so some pairings are less synergistic. |
| B13 | High: T3 concentration | Upgrading one favorite while others remain weak may dominate acquisition. | Let milestone ascension compete with card/relic rewards; early pledge and T2 guaranteed opportunity, not free duplicates. Compare single versus diversified builds. | Some players miss T3 if they take other rewards; preserve preview. |

## Divine Price assessment
v0.4 and v0.5 are distinct. Aurel's v0.4 temporary Strength/Dexterity penalty is avoidable in normal defensive builds; drop it in v0.5. Varran's healing conversion is real attrition pressure but irrelevant if no healing cards: keep as a trade-off in one law, not an additional penalty. Orun's 20% Attack loss can be circumvented with Stone Shatter (Skill, 11 divine damage) and Block effects; v0.5 remove blanket tax and move retaliation to cards, allowing lower damage tempo to emerge from choices. Mora's two Burdens can be fuel for Exhaust (v0.4 passive first two Exhausts do 3 AoE each); v0.5 drop mandatory Burdens and passive Exhaust damage, let Soulbound and sacrifice cards define price. Test oath opportunity costs before adding mandatory penalties. Dual-oath inherit actual rules, not removed prices.

## Proposed combat contract
- Persistent card instance ID, family, tier, god, rarity, pledge, ownership, zone, usage flags. One physical instance occupies one zone; temporary copies explicitly flagged. Normal and divine card ownership separate from combat location.
- Pledged card resolves mortal effects and transforms same instance to T1 even on lethal play, after resolution and before reward processing; no duplicate. Newly transformed card available immediately. No automatic repeated ascensions.
- Divine cards begin available in active Sanctums each combat; dormant cards preserved in their own inventory. Draw removes card from available; played/unplayed Sworn returns spent, unless Offered/Exhausted, Power or explicit effect. Refresh all spent active cards once at next turn start if nonempty normal discard was actually reshuffled since last refresh; no mid-turn refresh. Restore returns spent by default (specific effects can explicitly return available); never reset per-instance flags.
- Start turn ordinary budget 5; automatic divine slot consumes one of those five if eligible, otherwise normal fallback. Compare this cycle model to v0.4 every-turn return, v0.5 alternating, and retrieval-only. Draw from active eligible cards without replacement; explicit cross-oath effects may draw dormant, then return to dormant spent state. Selective Recall does not bypass spent unless stated. Never retrieve same instance twice per turn, even with explicit spent Recall.
- Resolve required costs/target validation before beneficial effects; insufficient Offering or status payment means no play (optional costs are marked). HP sacrifice bypasses Block/Anointed; prototype cannot pay lethal voluntary costs, and preview. Current resolving card not eligible for draw, Offer or Restore. Resolve effect order: pay costs -> replacements -> modifiers -> primary effects in printed order -> event triggers -> zone transition -> victory handling; no recursive conversion. Damage previews match committed result.
- Healing once per original instance per combat, including transformation, retrieval, restoration, and conversion; native divine damage is separate from converted healing. One Bloodlust per Attack, first hit; Resolve one per HP-reaching hit; Anointed one per hostile application; Soulbound one per Offer. Caps 3 are existing provisional values; don't add new caps without evidence. All combat buffs reset on encounter end.
- Energy-generating evolved Offering effect once per combat; card may still resolve other legal effects, with spent text. Restore disallows Powers, Curses, Restore-capable cards and non-Sanctum cards. Requiem counts distinct instance IDs that Exhausted this fight. No effect can create a copy of the resolving card unless explicitly authored. End combat returns persistent cards to run ownership, clears combat flags; no permanent HP/Strength gain unless explicitly authored. Ascension is run-only.
- Shrine event and rewards consume unique IDs; preview locked cards, accessible deck size, selected reward, tier and future access. No mid-combat oath change initially; first oath bonus once per run, no automatic farming from repeated allegiance. Twin Covenant shares automatic budget; v0.5 removed independent taxes remain removed.

## Validation experiments
Compare every-turn immediate return, alternating immediate return, retrieval-only, and cycle refresh (same seeds, deck rewards, enemies, tiers, relic restrictions). Test 1, 3, 5 divine cards; 8, 12, 16 ordinary cards; short 3–4 turn, elite 6–8, boss 10+ turn fights. No rare relic baseline, then targeted relic stress tests. Record HP loss/healing, win rate, turns, energy/card accesses, preferred-card share, number of plausible actions, empty draw events, unused cards, oath switch outcomes. Human testers rate clarity, excitement, meaningful decisions, frustration and desire to replay. Prototype targets: routine healing 0–3 HP, no closed resource loops, both Aurel/Varran viable with ordinary rewards. Investigate single-card strategy if it wins substantially more often across encounter types with comparable investment; don't nerf situational earned high rolls. No measurements exist yet.

## Implementation priority
P0 persistent identity and atomic zone transitions; draw model comparison; healing/energy use flags; costs/trigger ordering; deterministic test fixtures. P1 Aurel/Varran slice, reward cadence, switch preview, enemy escalation and playtests. P2 Orun/Mora, spent-retrieval exceptions, dual-oath relics, branching T3. Do not replace current stance code until a separate implementation decision. No game code changed by this document.
